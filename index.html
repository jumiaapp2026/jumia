Description

The Ecomax RF-430 is a spacious 430-litre side-by-side refrigerator designed for modern households that value both style and high functionality. It features No Frost technology, which circulates cool air throughout the compartments to maintain a consistent temperature and prevent ice build-up—eliminating the need for manual defrosting and ensuring food items stay fresher longer. Finished in an attractive Dark Silver color, the RF-430 adds a sleek, contemporary aesthetic to kitchen environments. The exterior is made of steel, contributing both to durability and ease of cleaning.Inside, the refrigerator is fitted with glass shelves for sturdiness, a water dispenser for convenience, and LED lighting that illuminates the interior brightly for better visibility and a modern feel. It holds a 3-star EPRA energy rating, making it relatively energy-efficient for its size, especially given its “fully tropicalized” design. The tropicalization implies the appliance is engineered to perform well in warmer climates without compromising cooling performance.
